# Statement of AI Assistance

I used OpenAI Codex throughout this project.

**Code.** Codex helped generate and revise portions of the Lean definitions,
theorem statements, proofs, and checks. Its assistance included debugging, from Tseitin encoding and finite model correspondence
to weighted counting, probability, sensitivity, monotonicity, and interval
bounds. Examples include `toCNF_correct` in `Tseitin/Completeness.lean`,
`weightedModelCount_eq_weightedCNFModelCount` in `Tseitin/Weighted.lean`, and
`failureProbability_cnf_interval_bounds` in `Tseitin/Intervals.lean`.

**Report.** Codex helped draft and revise the text, explain proof arguments,
develop examples, and edit the LaTeX source. It also helped prepare the
README and submission statements.
